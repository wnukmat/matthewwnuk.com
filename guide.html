Guide

Deep learning and robotics: a working guide

Fifteen short notes on the ideas behind today’s models and the robots they drive, written for engineers who are new to the field. They favour ideas over model names, because the names change every few months and the ideas do not.

15 topicsabout 47 minutes in totalDownload as slides (PDF)

Read in order, or jump to a topic. Each group builds on the one before it: how a network learns, which models exist, how they drive robots, and how the work is done.
